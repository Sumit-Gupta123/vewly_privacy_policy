Privacy Policy for Vewly

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

DevHub Studios ("we", "our", or "us") built the Vewly Android application as a free service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Vewly.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up with Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your full name, email address, unique user ID (UID), and selected role (Interviewer or Interview Giver).
  • Profile Information: Optional details you choose to add to your profile, including your headline, company or college, years of experience, skills, and short bio.
  • Session & Booking Data: Availability slots published by Interviewers (date, time window, duration, topic) and booking details when an Interview Giver books a slot.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To display Interviewer profiles and open availability slots to Interview Givers.
  • To schedule and manage mock interview bookings between users.
  • To maintain app security and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Third-Party Services

Vewly uses trusted third-party services provided by Google that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase Authentication (Email/Password and Google Sign-In)
  • Cloud Firestore (for securely storing user profiles and interview slot bookings)

You can review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Sharing & Visibility

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. If you register as an Interviewer, your name, headline, company, experience, skills, bio, and published slots are visible to authenticated Interview Givers within the app so they can book sessions with you.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at vishnugupta9571@gmail.com with the subject line "Vewly Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Vewly is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
